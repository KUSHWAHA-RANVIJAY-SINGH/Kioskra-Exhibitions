Exhibition Stall Cost in Delhi: Complete 2026 Price Breakdown

📅 July 15, 2026 ⏱ 8 min read ✍ Kioskra Team

If you're planning to exhibit at a trade show, expo, or industry event in Delhi, one of the first questions you'll ask is: "How much does an exhibition stall cost?"

The answer depends on several factors — booth size, design complexity, material quality, and the level of customization you need. In this comprehensive guide, we break down every cost component so you can plan your exhibition budget with confidence.

Understanding Exhibition Stall Pricing in Delhi

Exhibition stall costs in Delhi typically range from ₹1,200 to ₹5,000+ per square foot, depending on the type of setup. Here's a tier-by-tier breakdown:

1. Basic Setup: ₹1,200 – ₹1,800/sq ft

Best for startups and budget-conscious exhibitors who need a clean, professional presence. Basic setups include octanorm or shell-scheme structures with printed graphics, basic lighting, a counter, and flooring. This tier is ideal for first-time exhibitors testing the waters.

2. Premium Custom: ₹2,000 – ₹3,500/sq ft

The sweet spot for most brands. Premium booths feature fully custom-designed structures with high-quality materials like ACP panels, wood laminate, LED lighting systems, backlit signage, and dedicated meeting areas. This is what most returning exhibitors choose.

3. Luxury/Experiential: ₹4,000 – ₹5,000+/sq ft

For brands that want to make a statement. Luxury booths include double-decker structures, LED video walls, interactive touchscreen kiosks, AR/VR experiences, premium audio systems, and ultra-modern finishes like glass, metal, and acrylic.

What Factors Impact Exhibition Stall Cost?

Booth Size

Size is the primary cost driver. A 9 sqm (3×3) booth will cost significantly less than a 36 sqm (6×6) island booth. The industry standard minimum is usually 9 sqm at most major exhibitions like Pragati Maidan and India Expo Mart.

Design Complexity

Custom shapes, curves, double-height structures, and unique architectural elements increase fabrication complexity and cost. Simple rectangular layouts are more economical.

Material Quality

The choice between standard MDF, premium ACP panels, glass, metal, or sustainable materials significantly impacts both the visual quality and cost of your booth.

Technology Integration

LED screens, interactive kiosks, AR/VR setups, sound systems, and automated lighting controls add to the budget but dramatically increase visitor engagement.

Location & Venue

Costs vary between venues. Delhi exhibitions at Pragati Maidan, ITPO, or India Expo Mart have different logistics costs compared to Mumbai or Bangalore events.

How to Get the Best Value for Your Budget

  1. Start planning early — 60-90 days before the exhibition gives designers time to optimize your budget.
  2. Invest in reusable elements — Modular structures and reusable graphics save money across multiple events.
  3. Prioritize high-impact features — A single LED screen at the focal point outperforms 10 printed posters.
  4. Choose the right stall fabricator — Work with experienced companies (like Kioskra!) who understand venue regulations and can avoid costly on-site corrections.
  5. Consider total ROI — Don't just compare per-sqft rates. Factor in lead generation potential, brand perception, and visitor experience quality.
