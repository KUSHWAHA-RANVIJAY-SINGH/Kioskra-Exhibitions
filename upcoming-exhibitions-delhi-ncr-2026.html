Upcoming Exhibitions in Delhi NCR 2026-2027: Your Complete Guide to Pragati Maidan, Yashobhumi & India Expo Centre

📅 September 4, 2026 ⏱ 12 min read ✍ Kioskra Team

Delhi NCR is, without question, the exhibition capital of India. Between Bharat Mandapam (Pragati Maidan), Yashobhumi (IICC Dwarka), and the India Expo Centre & Mart in Greater Noida, the region hosts hundreds of trade fairs every year — covering everything from electronics and EVs to interiors, food, dairy, and defence.

If you’re a brand planning to exhibit in 2026, the hardest part usually isn’t the stall — it’s simply knowing which show to book, when, and how to register. So we put together a running list of the major exhibitions still ahead this year across all three venues, along with their official websites and a step-by-step exhibitor registration guide.

As a Delhi-based exhibition stall design and turnkey fabrication company, we build stalls across all three of these venues regularly — so this list is also the one we keep updated internally for our own clients.

Why Delhi NCR’s Exhibition Venues Matter for Your Brand

Each venue has its own personality:

  • Bharat Mandapam (Pragati Maidan) — India’s flagship government-run exhibition venue, home to the India International Trade Fair and dozens of ITPO-organised shows.
  • Yashobhumi (IICC), Dwarka — Asia’s newest and largest convention-cum-exhibition complex, built for large-scale international shows, right next to IGI Airport.
  • India Expo Centre & Mart, Greater Noida — the NCR’s dedicated B2B trade show hub, especially strong for manufacturing, interiors, EVs, and industrial sectors.

Step-by-Step Guide: How to Register as an Exhibitor

  1. Shortlist the right exhibition. Match your product category to the show’s audience.
  2. Visit the official website (listed above) and look for the “Exhibitor” section.
  3. Download the exhibitor manual/brochure for pricing, deadlines, and rules.
  4. Choose stall type: Raw/bare space (custom build) vs Shell scheme.
  5. Submit application form & deposit.
  6. Receive hall confirmation & venue guidelines.
  7. Brief your stall design & fabrication partner (Kioskra).
💡 Pro Tip: Start your registration process at least 8–12 weeks before your target show wherever possible to secure prime location and allow sufficient time for 3D booth design and approvals.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Which is the biggest exhibition venue in Delhi NCR?

Yashobhumi (IICC Dwarka) is Asia’s largest convention complex by area, while Bharat Mandapam (Pragati Maidan) hosts the highest footfall events like IITF.

Q: How early should I book a stall for IITF?

Ideally 3–4 months in advance as prime halls sell out fast.

Q: Does Kioskra work across all three venues?

Yes, we design and build turnkey stalls regularly at Bharat Mandapam, Yashobhumi, and India Expo Centre Greater Noida.
